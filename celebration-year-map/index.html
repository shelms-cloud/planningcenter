The one thing to know Easter moves
8 days earlier Apr 5, 2026 → Mar 28, 2027

What that cascades into

Holy Week is Mar 21–28, 2027. The whole Easter push moves up a week — promo that went out in mid-March this year needs to start in early March.

The Easter-focused First Wednesday moves from April into March — Mar 3, 2027 is the last First Wednesday before Easter. Easter planning that started mid-January this year should start in early January instead.

Two rhythms collide with Holy Week. Discover Celebration Pt. 2 would fall on Palm Sunday and Baptisms on Easter Sunday. Both are flagged in March below.

Gaps worth closing

Found while mapping
  1. Easter Sunday itself isn't an event on the calendar. Neither year. Everything around it is scheduled, but the service dates aren't blocked. Add: Apr 5, 2026 (past) · Palm Sun Mar 21, Good Fri Mar 26, Easter Mar 28, 2027
  2. No Christmas Eve or Christmas services on the calendar. Christmas Day 2027 is a Saturday, so Christmas Eve is a Friday — a clean evening-service slot. Add: Dec 24, 2027 (Friday) · Dec 26, 2027 falls the Sunday after
  3. Mother's Day and Father's Day are never blocked as service dates. Radiant's early-bird launch is pinned to Mother's Day, so the date matters operationally even beyond the service. 2027: Mother's Day May 9 · Father's Day Jun 20
  4. July 4, 2027 falls on a Sunday — and it's the 1st Sunday, so Discover Celebration Pt. 1 lands on it. Worth moving to Jul 11. 2026 had no equivalent conflict; Jul 4 was a Saturday
  5. Radiant is the only women's event on the calendar — early-bird launch on Mother's Day, conference in late September. There's planning time booked in February under a different name, which suggests a second women's moment that never got a date. 2027: early bird May 9 · conference Sep 24–25
Projection rules — fixed-weekday events keep their ordinal (1st Sunday stays 1st Sunday). Seasonal blocks keep their week-of-month. Easter-anchored items recomputed from Easter Mar 28, 2027. Mardi Gras recomputed from Fat Tuesday Feb 9, 2027. Rows tinted cobalt need a decision. Guest weekends carry no 2027 date — those get booked, not projected. Internal items on the shared calendar — staff meetings, planning and ideation sessions, blockouts, PTO, birthdays and facilities tickets — are excluded; this is the programming calendar only.
