Milestone steps

Some next steps feel like major moments: salvation, water baptism, joining the Dream Team, or leading a group.

Everyday steps

Others are just as essential to spiritual growth: starting a Bible-reading plan, attending church consistently, reconciling a relationship, or serving someone in everyday life.

Use this guide to start conversations that lead to personal growth and deeper faith — in group discussions, before or after serving, over coffee, or one-on-one. Share your own story and invite others to reflect on theirs. The goal is a culture where taking a next step feels natural and gets celebrated.

Next Steps In-Depth

Pick one or two for a conversation. You don’t need to ask every question.

Part 1 · Building Healthy Connection

1

Build Relationships by Sharing Personal Stories

Strong groups and teams start with strong connections. Create an environment where people feel seen and known.

Potential Questions

  • How long have you been part of Celebration Church?
  • Why did you choose this group or team?
  • What are you hoping God does in your life this season?
  • Icebreaker: If you could travel anywhere, where would it be and why?

Leader Tip: Take note of family members, stories, and details so you can pray specifically for each person.

2

Share Testimonies to Highlight Next Steps

Invite people to share their stories. Hearing how others have experienced God can spark growth.

Potential Questions

  • What stood out to you about this week’s testimony?
  • How are you hoping to grow in your relationship with God this season?
  • When did God become real to you?

Leader Tip: Encourage those who’ve taken big steps in their faith and celebrate their growth. Testimonies inspire others.

Share a Celebration Story
3

Accountability Through Healthy Relationships

We grow best together. Help your people see the value of biblical connection.

Potential Questions

  • What service do you typically attend?
  • Why is gathering with other believers important?
  • How can we support each other?

Leader Tip: Encourage people to check in on each other during the week and pray together.

Part 2 · Identifying Next Steps

4

Bible Reading

Help the people you lead build a habit of daily Bible reading.

Potential Questions

  • What Bible-reading plan do you use?
  • When and where do you read best?
  • How has Scripture impacted your life recently?

Leader Tip: Suggest a shared Bible plan for your group or team for accountability and encouragement.

Find a Bible Plan
5

Prayer

Make prayer a foundational part of your group or team culture.

Potential Questions

  • What does your daily prayer life look like?
  • Why do people sometimes feel uncomfortable praying out loud?
  • How can we grow in our prayer lives?

Leader Tip: Encourage people to join church-wide prayer opportunities, like our seasons of prayer and fasting.

Submit a Prayer Request
6

Influence: Making a Difference Daily

Help people recognize the impact they have on others.

Potential Questions

  • How can you apply what you’re learning to your daily life?
  • What opportunities do you have to encourage someone this week?
  • What role does forgiveness play in influencing others?

Leader Tip: Challenge your people to intentionally bring joy to their workplace, family, and neighborhood.

7

Water Baptism

Baptism is an outward expression of an inward change.

Potential Questions

  • Why is baptism an important step?
  • Have you been baptized? If not, what’s holding you back?
  • What does the Bible say about baptism?

Leader Tip: Encourage those who haven’t been baptized to take this step, and celebrate those who do.

Learn About Baptism Register for Baptism
8

Discover Celebration: Discover Purpose

Discover Celebration helps people understand their gifts and find where they fit and serve.

Potential Questions

  • What comes to mind when you hear the word “purpose”?
  • How do your gifts and talents point to your purpose?
  • Have you been through Discover Celebration? If so, what did you learn?

Leader Tip: Encourage your people to attend Discover Celebration — and go with them.

Part 3 · Continue Taking Steps

9

Developing Future Leaders

Help others see that leadership is simply influence.

Potential Questions

  • What makes someone a leader?
  • Where do you naturally have influence?
  • What’s holding you back from stepping into leadership?

Leader Tip: Let people lead prayer, share a devotional, help facilitate discussion, or train someone new on the team.

10

Encouraging Growth Beyond the Season

Faith is a lifelong journey, not just a semester or a serving rotation.

Potential Questions

  • What has God done in your life this season?
  • What’s your next step?
  • What group or team do you want to be part of next?

Leader Tip: Help people make a plan to keep growing after this season ends.

Find a Small Group Join the Dream Team
11

Encouraging Generosity

Generosity goes beyond financial giving — it’s a way of living that serves others.

Potential Questions

  • How has someone’s generosity impacted you?
  • In what ways can we be generous beyond finances?
  • Have you ever felt led to do something for someone but hesitated?

Leader Tip: Encourage people to look for opportunities to bless others this week.

Give Online
12

Encouraging Future Leaders

Some of the people you lead are future leaders. Help them take that step.

Potential Questions

  • What’s stopping you from leading?
  • How has this group or team impacted your life?
  • What kind of group would you want to lead, or what team would you want to help build?

Leader Tip: Have one-on-one conversations with potential leaders and invite them to leader training.
