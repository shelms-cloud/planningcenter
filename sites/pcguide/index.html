Ways to Care for Your People

You don’t have to do all of these. Pick what fits the moment and the person — and invite your group or team to help carry it.

Meal Trains

A Meal Train is a shared schedule where people sign up to bring meals over a set stretch of days. Set one up online in a few minutes, or pass around a simple sign-up sheet.

Start a Meal Train TakeThemAMeal

Flowers or Gifts

Small gestures — flowers, a gift card, a handwritten note — can bring real comfort in a hard season or help mark a milestone worth celebrating.

Encouragement

A text, a card, or a quick word on Sunday morning can stay with someone for a long time. A simple reminder that they are seen, valued, and prayed for makes a difference.

Celebrations

Birthdays, anniversaries, and personal wins are worth noticing. Recognizing them builds belonging and makes your group or team feel like family.

Prayer and Emotional Support

Taking time to listen, pray, and sit with someone in a hard moment is one of the most meaningful ways to care for them. Your presence matters more than having the right words.

Submit a Prayer Request

Always ask before sharing someone’s prayer request with your group, your team, or our prayer team.

Common Needs That May Arise

When you lead people week after week, you’ll be among the first to know when life gets hard.

Health Challenges

Support people facing surgery, illness, or injury by organizing a Meal Train, offering a ride, covering their serving spot, or helping with practical needs at home.

Start a Meal Train

Life Transitions

A new baby, an adoption, or welcoming a new family member can be joyful and overwhelming at the same time. Meals, encouragement, and a helping hand go a long way.

Loss and Grief

When someone loses a loved one or walks through another painful change, show up and keep showing up. Check in again in the weeks after, when things get quiet.

Spiritual Support

Some people are hungry to grow deeper; others are wrestling with their faith. Make room for honest conversation, pray together, and help them take their next step.

Reasons to Celebrate

Care isn’t only for hard days. Rejoice with the people you lead, too.

Milestones

Weddings, births, birthdays, anniversaries, graduations, and new jobs. Celebrating them together strengthens relationships.

Spiritual Growth

Notice breakthroughs and next steps — a decision to follow Jesus, a baptism, a new level of faith. Naming it encourages the person and everyone around them.

Register for Baptism

Answered Prayers

When God answers a prayer, stop and celebrate His faithfulness together. If it’s a story worth telling, we’d love to hear it.

Share a Celebration Story

Loop In a Pastor

You don’t carry this alone.

If someone you lead is hospitalized, loses a loved one, or is facing a crisis, let your Small Groups Coordinator or your Dream Team Coordinator or Captain know right away so our pastoral team can care for them alongside you.

When you care for people on purpose, you help build a church where everyone feels seen, valued, and encouraged in their faith.
