# Never Meant to Heal Alone — social share set

Instagram carousel (10 × 1080×1080) plus a story card (1080×1920) carrying the
devotional on chronic wounds, the interconnected body, and James 5:16.

All copy is verbatim from the original post. Only the line breaks, grouping, and
which lines make the cut are design decisions.

## Files

- `Main.dc.html` — card 01, cover
- `Slide02–Slide10.dc.html` — cards 02–10
- `Story.dc.html` — 9:16 story card
- `canvas.json` — artboard layout, titles, and sticky notes

`never-heal-alone.html` is the generated canvas (gitignored) — it is rebuilt from
these files, not edited directly.

Type follows the existing `data-type-vibe="editorial"` mode: `Georgia,
"Times New Roman", serif` at weight 400 with `-0.015em` tracking for statements
and scripture, `Helvetica, "Helvetica Neue", Arial, sans-serif` for body and
labels, and eyebrows at 700 / `0.22em` / uppercase.
