Different parts

A wound heals because countless systems throughout the body communicate with one another.

The brain and nerves sense the injury and send signals.

Blood and blood vessels deliver oxygen, nutrients, clotting factors, and immune cells to the site.

The immune system comes in to clean up what needs to be removed and protect the wound.

The lymphatic system helps drain fluid and clear what needs to go.

Different parts. Different jobs. One body.
