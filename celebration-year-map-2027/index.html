What changed in this copy

Baptisms are now the 2nd Sunday of every month, not the last. That fixes two collisions the old rule created — baptisms no longer land on Easter Sunday in March, or on the Sunday right after Christmas in December. It does create new overlaps in May, July, September and November; each one is marked below.

Men's One Night and the Radiant Women's Conference show TBD. Both are listed under the month they historically fall in so they hold their place, but neither has a date yet.

Easter is Mar 28, 2027 — eight days earlier than 2026. Holy Week is Mar 21–28, and the Easter-focused First Wednesday falls on Mar 3, so Easter planning needs to start in early January.

Decisions to make

Flagged while building this
  1. Men's One Night and Radiant both need dates. Radiant is the more urgent of the two: the early-bird launch is already pinned to Mother's Day, May 9, which means promo goes out roughly four months before the conference has a date. Old rules would give Aug 28 and Sep 24–25
  2. Three Sundays where a displaced event wants the 2nd Sunday that Baptisms now holds. Discover Celebration Pt. 2 needs to move off Palm Sunday (Mar 21) and the natural slot is Mar 14. Pt. 1 needs to move off July 4 and the natural slot is Jul 11. Pt. 1 on Sep 5 is Labor Day weekend — 2026 moved it a week for exactly that reason, which would be Sep 12. All three land on Baptisms. Mar 14 · Jul 11 · Sep 12 — pick which rhythm yields
  3. May 9 is carrying four things. Mother's Day, the Radiant early-bird launch, Small Group Leader Training, and now Baptisms. Worth deciding what comes off. Nov 14 is doubled too — Baptisms plus the final Legacy Sunday
  4. Easter Sunday itself isn't an event on the calendar. Everything around it is scheduled, but the service dates aren't blocked. Add: Palm Sun Mar 21 · Good Fri Mar 26 · Easter Mar 28
  5. No Christmas Eve or Christmas services on the calendar. Christmas Day 2027 is a Saturday, so Christmas Eve is a Friday — a clean evening-service slot. Add: Dec 24, 2027 (Friday)
  6. Mother's Day and Father's Day are never blocked as service dates. Both now carry weight beyond the service. Mother's Day May 9 · Father's Day Jun 20
  7. July 4 falls on a Sunday and it's the 1st Sunday, so Discover Celebration Pt. 1 lands on it. Independence Day, Sun Jul 4, 2027
  8. Two missions teams are out at the same time in July. Honduras Medical Mission (Jul 17–24) overlaps the CY Boston Trip (Jul 18–24). Same overlap existed in 2026
  9. Spring break dates need confirming. The CY Texas trip is built on Caddo/Bossier spring break week. Currently projected Mar 7–11
Dates derived from the 2026 programming calendar and projected forward — fixed-weekday events keep their ordinal, seasonal blocks keep their week-of-month, Easter-anchored items recomputed from Easter Mar 28, 2027, Mardi Gras from Fat Tuesday Feb 9, 2027. Baptisms recomputed to the 2nd Sunday of each month. Olive tint means a decision is needed; cobalt means no date yet. Dotted bars in the calendar are the continuing days of a multi-day trip or season. Guest weekends aren't included — those get booked, not projected. Internal items on the shared calendar — staff meetings, planning and ideation sessions, blockouts, PTO, birthdays and facilities tickets — are excluded; this is the programming calendar only.
